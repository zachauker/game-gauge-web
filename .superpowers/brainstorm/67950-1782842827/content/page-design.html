Key behaviour notes

  • Genre chip carries through to IGDB search — e.g. "RPG" + "witcher" filters IGDB results to RPG games
  • Platform & Sort chips are disabled in search mode — greyed out with a tooltip. IGDB search is relevance-ordered.
  • Curated rows disappear when search is active — replaced by a flat IGDB result grid
  • Community ratings overlay shown on cards already in your DB; others show a subtle "+ add" affordance
  • /browse and /search routes retire — both redirect to /games (or /discover)
  • Genre browse (/browse/genre/:genre) retires — clicking "RPG" genre chip on the unified page is equivalent
